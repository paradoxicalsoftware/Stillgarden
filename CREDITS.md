# Credits and asset provenance

Stillgarden is a labor of love by [Paradoxical Software](https://paradoxicalsw.com), founded by Giamo Lao. Development and visual iteration were AI-assisted. We welcome contributors who make it more thoughtful, accessible and reliable.

## Code and original assets

The native app is built with SwiftUI, AppKit and AVFoundation. Flowers, pollinators, lighting, rain, lightning and accumulated snow are procedural graphics. The app icon is an original, code-drawn botanical mark. Code and original project assets are offered under the MIT license in this repository.

`Resources/garden.png` is an AI-generated painted backdrop created with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. It is not a photograph. The prompt requested a wide, serene miniature garden clearing in gouache, with moss in the foreground, ferns at the edges, misty sage trees, warm sunlight, an empty planting bed, and no text or people. No external reference image was used.

Gallery images in `docs/assets/` use the actual SwiftUI view code with staged gardens. They are generated with the `--render-demo` command and do not contain a user's garden data.

## Recorded owl

**Owl Hoot** by **Breviceps**, Freesound sound **465697**.

- [Original recording and license](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/465697/)
- [CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Source preview: `SourceAudio/owl-source.mp3`
- Playback asset: `Resources/owl-call.wav`

The recording was decoded, filtered to reduce low-frequency handling rumble, level-normalized and given short edge fades. Its pitch and original call timing are retained. CC0 does not require attribution, but we are grateful to the recordist and include it here.

## Synthesized sound

Seasonal bird and cricket beds, rain, wind and the three thunder variants are generated by the included Python scripts. They are stylized sound design rather than field recordings. No external audio service or download is required to run the app.

## Botanical references

Illustrated growth habits were informed by [RHS lavender guidance](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/lavender) and [OSU sunflower varieties](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/em-9866-grow-your-own-sunflowers). Those references are not bundled assets. Growth rates and lifecycles remain intentionally accelerated and simplified.
