# Owl recording

**Owl Hoot** by **Breviceps**, Freesound sound 465697.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/465697/
- Public preview used: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/465/465697_9159316-hq.mp3
- License: CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Source describes a real hooting owl recorded by mobile phone.
- Adaptation: decoded to 22.05 kHz stereo, low-frequency handling rumble reduced, level normalized, edge fades applied. The call's pitch and timing are preserved.

Other sound layers and thunder variants are procedurally generated by the included scripts.
