# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-29

First public release of Stillgarden by Paradoxical Software.

- Native pinned desktop garden with edge-revealed controls and optional visits.
- Ten procedural flower varieties, growth speeds and per-plant controls.
- Bees, caterpillars, cocoons and butterflies with nighttime rest.
- Smooth clock-following, manual and accelerated day/night lighting.
- Local weather and seasons, snow accumulation/melt, randomized lightning and delayed thunder.
- Seasonal soundscapes, a recorded CC0 owl call, mute and volume controls.
- Local saves, offline progress, reset and persistent undo.
- God mode via a triple-click in the sky.
- Public source, contribution guidance, community policies and project website.

Known release limits: ad-hoc signing rather than notarization; Intel is cross-compiled but not yet verified on Intel hardware; accessibility and dense-garden performance need broader community testing. Weather and plant development are stylized simulations, not forecasts or biological models.
