# Community Code of Conduct

Stillgarden should be a welcoming place to make something gentle together. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions or project events is expected to follow this code, including maintainers.

## Our standard

Treat people with dignity regardless of age, disability, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, appearance, experience or background. Be curious, give useful feedback, and assume that you may be missing context. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Respect a person's boundaries and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate intimidation and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Do not pressure others to disclose health information, offer unsolicited diagnoses, or treat the project as a place to judge someone's mental health. No one owes the community a personal story.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to **contact@paradoxicalsw.com**, with “Stillgarden conduct” in the subject. Include relevant links and context if you feel comfortable doing so. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers will review reports discreetly, limit sharing to what is needed to respond, and avoid retaliation. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when safety or legal obligations require disclosure.

If a concern involves a maintainer, say so in the report; a person directly involved should not be the sole reviewer. You may also use GitHub's own reporting tools. This project cannot provide emergency support.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude someone for severe or repeated violations. Responses should consider the impact, context and pattern of behavior. When appropriate, explain the reason and how to appeal privately through the same contact address.

This policy applies to project spaces and to public behavior when someone is representing the project. Good-faith criticism and respectful disagreement are welcome.
